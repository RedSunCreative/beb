Little Big Voices LIVE Guest Welcome Kit: Episode 12!

Welcome to Little Big Voices LIVE!

What This Show Is About: Little Big Voices creates understanding, compassion, and connection through authentic conversations and performances. We explore the fabric of our shared humanity — discovering that we have more in common than we have differences. This isn't about perfection or performance; it's about being genuinely seen and heard.

 

Why You're Here: Mark invited you because often Spirit will whisper to him who to invite - and often because your story/energy/presence strikes a chord in him—you inspire him and strengthen him as he lives out his story and mission. Also, sharing your experience may do the same for our audience as they navigate their own journeys. Your voice and story matter, and we're honored to hold space for you to express it.

The Theme: Little Big Gratitude

We’ll be exploring what happens when some real people from different backgrounds, cultures, and beliefs get together to deeply listen to each other and contemplate our wondering question for this episode: “What am I grateful for now, that at the time, I did not see as a gift?” You may or may not share your response on the show - that’s up to you - but just wondering it will be the only preparation you need!
